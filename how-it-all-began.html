06 / The Question

Every night, I found myself wondering how this app made its choice — out of every rider on that street, at that minute, why me? Why not someone else?

Not once. Not as a passing thought. Every shift, every restaurant, every idle minute waiting for the next ping.

From the saddle → the spreadsheet

This is how the data
of those 14 months
became a portfolio.

Seven chapters. Every claim from those years tested against the GDPR export of my own rider account. Sometimes the data agreed with me. Often, it did not.
