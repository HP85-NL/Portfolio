03 / The bonus Uber never sent

Do 50, get 150.

In August 2021 Deliveroo dropped a challenge into the app: complete 50 orders during "Superpeak" hours in one week, get €150 on top. I hit it. Uber never sent me anything like it in 14 months.

04 / Two mental models of the same job

Uber sent pings.
Deliveroo sold shifts.

On Uber I answered a ping, delivered, waited for the next. Sessions were short bursts. On Deliveroo I clocked into a "session," stayed on for hours, and picked up whatever came in. Two completely different rhythms of the same work.

06 / What the numbers didn't decide

Deliveroo paid me better per order, gave me bonuses Uber never sent, and treated the work like a shift instead of a ping. Then in 2022, Deliveroo exited the Netherlands.

The platform that treated me better didn't survive. The one that counted trips instead of hours did. Platform economics is not a formula — it's a set of choices about what to measure, who to reward, and how long a company thinks it can keep doing both.

That's the moment I understood I wasn't a delivery rider anymore. I was reading a dataset.
